## Goal and assessment of the existing portfolio

The owner’s direction is a personal portfolio with a friendly willingness to
help. Conversion here means a visitor explores real work, understands the
owner’s approach, and starts a relevant conversation. It is not a paid-service
checkout or a sales funnel. No conversion baseline, traffic data, or measured
uplift is available.

| Observed friction                                                           | Change                                                                                       | Intended effect                                                 |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Project links required reading long descriptive paragraphs                  | Two distinct project cards with a question, approach, tools, dashboard, and source-code link | Make evidence easier to assess and explore                      |
| The hero described many interests before giving visitors a route to contact | Shorter introduction with work and conversation links                                        | Explain the portfolio quickly and offer an optional next step   |
| Role, background, and experience were scattered                             | Compact linked context band                                                                  | Make the person behind the portfolio easier to understand       |
| Methods were mainly on deeper pages                                         | Three-step approach beside the work                                                          | Show how the owner thinks without claiming unsupported outcomes |
| Contact invitation was broad                                                | Optional topic-specific email links alongside the general email address                      | Reduce the effort of writing a first message                    |
| The closing invitation had little visual separation                         | Bordered, spacious invitation panel                                                          | Give the end of the page a clear next step                      |

## Implementation boundaries

Keep the existing portrait, blue-and-paper visual identity, themes, genuine
projects, verified employer information, and search foundations. No testimonials,
client logos, revenue results, availability commitments, or response-time
promises have been invented. No urgency prompts, forced forms, or budget
qualification have been added. Email topic links open a visitor’s email app;
they do not submit data or send a message automatically.

## Validation and measurement

Validate phone and desktop layouts in both themes, link targets, email subjects,
keyboard access, existing tests, static export integrity, and performance budgets.
A clearer design is a hypothesis, not proof of more enquiries.

Once GA4 is configured and an appropriate consent approach is established,
consider deliberate events for dashboard opens, source-code opens, contact-page
visits, and email-link clicks. Treat an email click as intent, not a sent email
or qualified conversation. Compare engagement and actual relevant conversations
over a representative period; exclude the owner’s own testing. No analytics
account or event tracking has been configured by this change.
